Historical period: Paleolithic 
Artwork used as inspiration: Cave paintings, Pech Merle (c. 23,000 BCE)
Visual Prompt: A Paleolithic cave painting made with natural pigments like black charcoal and red ochre on the rough, uneven limestone wall of a dark cave. The composition uses bold contour lines to show wild spotted horses in profile alongside negative human handprints created by blowing pigment over hands pressed against the rock. The natural bumps and rough texture of the stone wall are used to give volume and scale to the animals, with no background or ground line, lit only by the warm light of a torch. Created by nomadic hunter-gatherers as part of a spiritual ritual rather than home decoration, the purpose of this artwork was to ask for success in hunting, respect animal spirits, and ensure the group's survival in a harsh prehistoric world.








Historical theme: Symbolic representation of the divine 
Historical period: Byzantine 
Artwork used as inspiration: Christ Icon, St. Catherine`s Monastery 
Visual Prompt: A Byzantine religious icon painted with encaustic wax on a wooden panel, showing a sacred bearded figure from the chest up in a strict, frontal pose. Instead of realistic human proportions, the artwork uses strong outlines in the dark robes, large serious eyes looking straight at the viewer, a glowing halo, and a flat gold background that removes any sense of real physical space to represent heaven. Made during the Byzantine Empire for personal prayer and worship, this painting was not meant to be a normal portrait, but a spiritual bridge to connect believers with God, reflecting a culture that valued religious faith and divine power over physical realism.
